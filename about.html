Our story

Digambar Jain Social Group, Kota Main was established in 2003. It is a group of Digambar Jain families of Kota who meet as one family, celebrate festivals together and stand by each other in good and difficult times.

Our aim is simple: service to society, good values for the next generation, and bringing families closer. Through the year we hold religious, cultural and social programmes, family get-togethers and service activities.

Our values

ServiceHelping society through service activities like blood donation and support to the needy.
Good valuesPassing Jain values and traditions to our children.
TogethernessStanding with each other like one family.
